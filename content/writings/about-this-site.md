This website is meant to be a multipurpose place for being a portfolio, digital diary and much more.
I'll write stuff here about the software I use, the projects I'm planning or working on, the tools I like and how I configured them and a lot more.

There's only one button in the navbar to keep things simple. If you're on a keyboard, press / or Ctrl+K to fuzzy find anything on the site, or press g to jump around with vim motions (gh for home, gp for projects, gw for writings, gn for now, and gf for photography).
